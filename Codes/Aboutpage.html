About

INGREDIENTS

The restaurant prides itself on using ingredients that are rare, expensive, and often hard to source. These include ingredients that only a few chefs in the world can obtain or prepare properly. Their presence in the story adds to the sense of exclusivity and luxury.

Some key ingredients:

  • White truffles from Italy (prized by the gram)
  • Bluefin tuna belly (a delicacy, very scarce, and extremely rare and pricey)
  • Caviar from rare sturgeon (Beluga or Almas)
  • Matsutake mushrooms (rare and fragrant, highly exclusive in Japan)
  • Kopi Luwak coffee (made from coffee beans the digestive system of civets)
  • Edible gold leaf (used for decoration, symbolizing luxury)
